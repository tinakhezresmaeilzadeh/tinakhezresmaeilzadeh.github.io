MORFI: Multimodal Zero-Shot Reasoning for Financial Time-Series Inference

Tina Khezresmaeilzadeh, Parsa Razmara, Mohammad Erfan Sadeghi, Seyedarmin Azizi, Erfan Baghaei Potraghloo

University of Southern California

PaperSupplementaryCode

Abstract

MORFI is a training-free framework that uses vision-language models (VLMs) to predict stock prices by combining line-chart images with numerical time-series data. Prompting the VLM with both modalities, optionally with chain-of-thought reasoning, outperforms text-only LLMs by 40–80% in MSE.
